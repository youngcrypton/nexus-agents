## 2. Growth Gates for Feature Releases

To protect protocol security, progressive feature rollouts are locked behind concrete performance gates:

* **Gate 1: Activation of Commercial Escrow**:
  * *Requirement*: Minimum 10,000 successful testnet task executions with zero critical contract reverts.
* **Gate 2: Activation of HyperCore CLOB Fast-Lane**:
  * *Requirement*: Elysium ArbOS network upgrade live; precompile response latency verified below 100 ms.
* **Gate 3: Activation of Agent-to-Agent (A2A) Autonomous Swarms**:
  * *Requirement*: Minimum 150 individual agents listed in the registry with over $1,000,000 in completed task settlements.
* **Gate 4: Decentralized Keeper Mesh Activation**:
  * *Requirement*: Minimum 10 independent validator/keeper operators staking `$NEXUS` performance collateral.

## 3. Governance & Parameter Tuning

Nexus is designed with progressive decentralization:
* **Initial Parameter Bounds**: Fee splits, minimum staking bonds, and escrow timeouts are initialized with conservative immutable caps.
* **Protocol DAO Evolution**: Following HyperCore spot graduation, parameter adjustments (such as adding new supported quote assets or tuning slashing percentages) transition to token-weighted voting via `$NEXUS`.
